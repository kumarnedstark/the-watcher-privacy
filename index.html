The Watcher Saga – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 (no tracking; renamed)

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play The Watcher Saga on iPhone or iPad (the “app”). The app is made by Sivakumar Nedumaran, an individual developer (“I”, “me”).

The short version: the app has no accounts and no servers of its own. I don't collect, see or store your personal information. Ads in the free version are provided by Google AdMob. They are not personalised and the app does not track you; Google may still collect some data to show and measure ads, as described below.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress (stars earned per level and difficulty), settings, and whether you bought “Remove Ads” are saved on your device only. They are never sent to me. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads (watch an ad to continue a level). These are served by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device information (model, operating system, language);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps), and crash or performance data from the ad software.

Your choices:

  • No tracking: the app never asks to track you and does not use your device's advertising identifier. Every ad is non-personalised: it is not chosen based on your activity in other apps or websites.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states): the app shows Google's consent message before personalised ads are used. You can review or change your choice at any time from the app's menu (Privacy settings).
  • No ads at all: buying “Remove Ads” turns off all ads in the app.

Google's handling of this data is covered by Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

“Remove Ads” is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; I never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple only tells the app that the purchase was made, so it can remove ads and restore the purchase on your other devices. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for teens.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California and other US states), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the use of your data, and to withdraw consent. Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access or delete; you can change your ad consent in the app's Privacy settings, reset your advertising identifier in iOS, or contact Google about data used for ads. You can also contact me with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
